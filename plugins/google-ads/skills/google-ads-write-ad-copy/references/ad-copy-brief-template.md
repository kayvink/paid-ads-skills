# Ad copy brief template

Fill one brief per ad group (or PMax asset group) before writing any line. The brief is channel-neutral substance: the same angles, proof and voice can later feed other formats. The RSA or PMax copy is rendered from it under the format's limits; the brief itself never carries character counts.

Show the filled brief to the user before writing. A brief that can't name one motion and one primary metric isn't ready: ask, don't guess.

```markdown
---
ad_group: <campaign> / <ad group>
account: <account name>
mode: new | refresh | pmax
motion: <the one motion this campaign serves, from ads/taxonomy.md>
primary_metric: <what this copy is graded on, e.g. trial signups>
final_url: <landing page>
lp_verified: true | false
created: YYYY-MM-DD
---

## Audience
Who searches these queries and what they are trying to get done, in one or two sentences.

## Query language
The buyer's own words, from converting search terms and the keywords carrying 80% of spend:
- <term> (<conversions>, <spend>)
- ...

## What the landing page promises
- Headline / H1: <text>
- Main call to action (button text): <text>
- Offer and its conditions: <e.g. 14-day free trial, no card required>
- Prices shown: <exact strings>

## Proof we can use
Only items shown on the landing page or recorded in wiki/company.md, each with its source:
- <claim> (source: <LP section or wiki/company.md>)

## Angles available
- Keyword: <themes>
- Benefit: <outcomes, not features>
- Proof: <from the list above>
- Offer: <from the landing page>
- CTA: <matches the page's main button>
- Objection to answer: <switching cost, setup time, price, trust>

## Voice
- Tone: <from wiki/company.md>
- Case style: Title Case | sentence case
- Words we use: <list>
- Words we never use: <list, plus memory entries>
- Required disclaimers or pins: <from memory, or none>

## Keep from the current ad (refresh mode)
- <asset text> (BEST | GOOD)

## Replace (refresh mode)
- <asset text> (LOW): <why, and the angle the replacement takes>
```
